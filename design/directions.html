The shield, and “I prayed”

This is the acquisition-hook candidate, behind a feature flag. It is also the most constrained screen in the app: a ManagedSettings shield may contain a static title, subtitle, icon and two buttons. Nothing else. No countdown, no live data, no deep link. “I prayed” sets an App-Group flag that the app clears on next open — which is why the second screen exists.

What I need from you

  • Pick per surface, not overall. They are built from one token set, so “A’s home with B’s shield” is a real answer and costs nothing.
  • The Lock Screen widget is not a choice — iOS renders it monochrome. The only decision there is which number is largest: clock time (A) or countdown (B).
  • Arabic on this page is unverified. Every Arabic string and the Hijri date are listed in REVIEW.md for the Muslim reviewer. The German line is Bubenheim & Elyas and must be checked byte-for-byte against QuranEnc deu_frankbubenheima 1.1.0 before it ships.
  • Prayer times shown are illustrative Berlin values, not computed by adhan-js yet.
TikTok legibility. Both directions clear the 1080×1920 text box (x 130–950, y 230–1440): the largest figure on each home screen is the countdown, which lands at roughly 18% of frame height when filmed. B has more headroom after re-encode — amber on #0B0F14 is a far bigger contrast step than green on bone. If the lock is the hook the marketing test picks, that matters; if the widget is the hook, they are level.
